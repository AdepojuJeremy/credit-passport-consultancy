# Public Content Source Policy

The consultancy website separates four kinds of public material:

1. **Service descriptions** — statements of what CreditPassport is prepared to do in an engagement. These are capability statements, not performance claims.
2. **Research notes** — practitioner frameworks derived from internal CreditPassport architecture, decision-system and research documents. They must be labelled as research notes unless external evidence has been independently reviewed.
3. **Engagement patterns** — anonymized descriptions of recurring client problems and consulting approaches. They may describe context and method, but not client identity or outcome metrics without permission and evidence.
4. **Case studies** — published only when client context, intervention, evidence, outcome definitions and publication permission have been reviewed.

## Claims rule

A numerical improvement, client result, market statistic or causal statement must not appear as an achieved CreditPassport result unless the underlying evidence is available and the claim has been reviewed for publication.

Internal MVP targets such as approval lift at a comparable default rate are design and validation targets. They are not achievements until supported by measured outcomes.

## Confidentiality rule

Do not expose client-identifying operating details, datasets, decision rules, thresholds or internal documents unless explicit publication permission exists.

## Research rule

Internal architecture can support conceptual research notes. It cannot by itself establish empirical validity, predictive power or causal impact. Public research should distinguish framework, hypothesis, internal design target and externally validated finding.
